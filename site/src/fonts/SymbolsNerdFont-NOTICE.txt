SymbolsNerdFontMono-subset.woff2 is a subset of Symbols Nerd Font Mono 3.5.0
(https://www.nerdfonts.com), Copyright (c) 2016 Ryan McIntyre, MIT License.
It keeps six glyphs, used in the terminal prompt on the page:

- U+E0B0, U+E0B6  powerline separators (Nerd Fonts / Powerline Extra Symbols, MIT License)
- U+F07C, U+F00D  folder open and times (Font Awesome by Fonticons, Inc., SIL Open Font License 1.1)
- U+F418          git branch (Octicons by GitHub, Inc., MIT License)
